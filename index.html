Focus

Sectors with structural tailwinds.

Cloud Global Group works with companies operating at the intersection of how people live, work, move, protect themselves, and stay well. The emphasis is not breadth for its own sake, but a set of sectors where technology, distribution, and behavior are all shifting at once.

The current lens spans healthtech, insurtech, traveltech, and the future of work, with selective exposure to sportstech where data, performance, and experience are converging in new ways.

HealthtechConsumer health, digital care experiences, enablement, and new models of access.
InsurtechEmbedded protection, modern distribution, product strategy, and customer trust.
TraveltechBooking, distribution, ancillary revenue, loyalty, and globally mobile users.
Future of workTools, services, and communities supporting distributed teams and modern professionals.

Approach

Built as a venture platform, not a traditional consultancy.

The model is intentionally simple: work closely with a small number of companies, contribute where strategy and execution meet, and stay anchored in the problems that appear earliest in a venture’s life. That includes sharper customer definition, product framing, monetization logic, and founder-level decision support.

Cloud Global Group also develops and holds ideas of its own. Over time, the platform is designed to be a home for advisory work, studio initiatives, and selective investment activity tied together by a consistent point of view.

Building a portfolio around the future of health, protection, mobility, work, and performance.
