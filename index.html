01 How it works

Three steps. That’s the whole model.

No team grid, no maze of services. One clear way in — and a conversation only if it’s a fit.

1

Submit your deck — or just tell us about it

Share a deck link, upload your PDF or slides, or choose “No deck yet” and describe the business in your own words. Tell us, in one line, what help you actually want.

2

We read it ourselves

After you submit the form, our team reads your deck — or your description, if you didn’t send one — and returns it as a scorecard: narrative, market, model, GTM, team, and ask, with the top three fixes to make first.

3

Talk, only if it’s a fit

The review is returned to the email you provide. If it points to working together, we talk about what happens next — funding or an advisory partnership. If not, you still keep the review. No obligation either way.

03 Working together

Two ways we work together.

If it’s a fit, there are two ways forward:

1

Funding

We help you find funding, or we fund you ourselves — backing the company with capital so it can take its next step.

2

Advisory partner

We become your advisory partner — staying close on positioning, go-to-market, and your revenue engine as you grow.

Neither outcome is promised or guaranteed. The review is free, advisory feedback only — and if neither path is a fit, you still keep the review, with no obligation.

07 About & contact

A small team. A clear point of view.

Cloud Global Ventures is a small team of go-to-market and partnerships operators and advisors to early-stage ventures. The work is deliberately small and close: a handful of companies at a time, at the stage where customer definition, product framing, monetisation logic, and founder-level decisions matter most.

Zero to one, with purpose: the filter isn’t sector or stage — it’s conviction. Founders who know why this problem, and why them, get help refining business models, go-to-market, and revenue engines so they’re pitch-ready for a first check.
